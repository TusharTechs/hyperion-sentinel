EXECUTIVE SUMMARY This deliverable presents the design, implementation and validation of the softwarized infrastructure and containerization in the form of Open Connectors for the HYPER-AI cloud infrastructure. These Open Connectors are responsible for managing edge devices in the form of cloud-edge resources including edge devices which can be (but not limited to) smartphones and tablets, XR equipment such as smart glasses and any set of devices with different operating systems including among other Linux-based OS and Android. This deliverable, D4.2, documents all the design and implementation aspects of the connectors which form a framework for the registration and management of multimodal edge devices as cloud nodes.

The document begins by describing what the Open Connectors are and what they contribute to the HYPER-AI cloud. An architectural overview of the connectors is described and how there are used as a layer of abstraction between the cloud and heterogeneous and diverse devices as well as how they extend the Kubernetes-native operations and configuration. The open connectors consist of smaller components for registering the edge devices and establishing communication channels towards them. The edge devices, the Device Nodes, are managed by the Open Connectors and are utilized as cloud nodes from the cloud infrastructure.

A detailed description of the development of the Open Connectors is documented in this deliverable to support and explain the design and implementation decisions and methodology applied for implementing all required functionalities. The goal of these connectors is to enable the HYPER-AI cloud infrastructure to manage edge devices as cloud nodes and schedule applications across a cloud-edge-IoT continuum.

The deliverable concludes with an initial implementation roadmap towards version 1.0.0 of the Open Connectors, which is reported as an open-source module under Apache License 2.0.

In this version all described functionalities are implemented, tested and validated across the HYPER-AI cloud infrastructure. The implementation is followed by a detailed online documentation.
