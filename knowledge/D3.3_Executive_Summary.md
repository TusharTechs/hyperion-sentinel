EXECUTIVE SUMMARY This deliverable presents the architectural specifications of the HYPER-AI platform. HYPER-AI is a hyper-distributed, self-managed system designed to enable intelligent orchestration of computing, storage, and communication resources across the cloud-edge-IoT continuum. The architecture defines how diverse computational assets, that range from cloud-based resources to constrained IoT devices, can be abstracted, registered, discovered, and dynamically orchestrated under a common control framework.

The document begins by outlining the methodology and high-level design principles that guide the architecture, including computational abstraction, Kubernetes-native orchestration, distributed trust and security via Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), and self- configuration, self-healing, self-optimization and self-protection (self-CHOP) autonomy. It also introduces the conceptual model of the computing continuum deigned to align with real- world use cases across the five vertical domains of the project:

Manufacturing, Energy, Agriculture, Automotive, and Healthcare Subsequent sections provide a detailed breakdown of the system’s layered architecture, encompassing infrastructure-level abstraction, open connectors, and intelligent orchestration mechanisms for workload placement and optimization. The architecture includes dedicated modules for swarm formation, federated learning, distributed resource optimization, and intrusion detection. It also incorporates a flexible administration and development environment.

The deliverable concludes with an initial implementation roadmap and lessons learned during the design phase. It serves as the architectural foundation that will guide the technical development activities in the next project phases.
